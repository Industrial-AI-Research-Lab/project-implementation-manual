# Industrial Project — Guidance for Supervisors

This document supplements the general requirements. In an industrial project, the supervisor must connect the work to a real process and ensure that its impact can be measured.

## Before approving the topic

Define:

- the industrial customer or user;
- the specific process and pain point;
- the existing solution or current process as a baseline;
- measurable impact criteria;
- data availability and feasibility of evaluation;
- deployment constraints; and
- an industry representative who can provide a substantive review.

The industrial topic is formulated by an industry representative acting as the customer.

## Metrics

Depending on the task, suitable measures extend beyond ML metrics and may include:

- reduced process time;
- reduced labor requirements;
- processing or inference cost;
- increased throughput;
- reduced failure rate;
- improved decision accuracy;
- economic impact; and
- reduced computational or organizational resource use.

## What to monitor during the project

- the solution is compared with the current process, not only with another model;
- the user or production scenario remains realistic;
- data and infrastructure constraints are recorded explicitly;
- the technical prototype is sufficient to demonstrate the claimed impact; and
- the industry review is not prepared as a formality on the final day, but is based on a demonstration or results.

## Critical grading risks

- missing mandatory industry review limits the grade to 4B;
- missing quantitative impact and comparison invokes the general cap of 4C;
- a hypothetical claim that the result “could be deployed” without analyzing constraints does not demonstrate high deployment potential; and
- a project without a real or realistic user and process loses the defining purpose of the industrial type.

## What the final result must communicate

The committee must quickly understand: **who the user is → what problem they face → how it is currently solved → what is proposed → what measurable impact it has → how realistic deployment is → how the customer confirms these claims.**
