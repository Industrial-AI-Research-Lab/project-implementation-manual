# Scientific Project — Guidance for Supervisors

This document supplements the general requirements. The supervisor's main responsibility is to prevent a research problem from being replaced by a purely technical implementation task.

## When defining the topic

Establish the following chain in advance:

**relevant scientific problem → limitation of known methods → research question or hypothesis → expected scientific contribution → method for testing the hypothesis.**

The project must be designed so that, by the defense, its difference from existing approaches can be demonstrated quantitatively or theoretically.

## What to define in the project statement

- a specific research question or hypothesis;
- what exactly constitutes scientific novelty;
- strong relevant baselines and possible state-of-the-art approaches;
- the primary dataset or tasks and additional robustness checks;
- metrics and the statistical or methodological rationale;
- an ablation study or component analysis if the method has multiple components;
- criteria for considering the hypothesis supported or unsupported; and
- a preprint plan as the final scientific artifact.

## What to monitor during the project

By the midpoint, the project should preferably have a working baseline, a reproducible experimental pipeline, and at least an initial comparison. This makes it possible to determine in time whether there is a scientific effect instead of discovering its absence immediately before the defense.

A negative result is acceptable when the hypothesis, experimental design, and analysis are sound. Merely running several models without drawing a research conclusion is not.

## Critical grading risks

- without quantitative comparison, 5A is impossible;
- without a substantive baseline, the maximum grade is 3D;
- without component analysis for a complex method, the maximum is 4C;
- an interesting hypothesis without adequate testing does not warrant a high grade; and
- if the student does not understand the methodology or experiment, strong artifacts do not compensate for that.

## Final review

Before the defense, the problem statement, hypothesis, method, experiment, metrics, statistical conclusions, limitations, and claimed novelty must be mutually consistent. The preprint must be structured as a scientific paper rather than an extended technical report.
